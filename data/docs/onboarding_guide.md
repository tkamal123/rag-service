# Acme Analytics Onboarding Guide

New workspaces start with a 14-day free trial that includes all Pro features. No credit card is required to start the trial.

To connect your first data source, open Settings, choose Integrations, and select a connector such as PostgreSQL, BigQuery or Google Sheets. The first sync usually completes in under 10 minutes for datasets smaller than 1 GB.

Every workspace can invite up to 5 team members on the Starter plan and up to 50 on the Pro plan. Workspace owners can assign one of three roles: Viewer, Editor or Admin. Only Admins can manage billing and delete data sources.

Dashboards refresh automatically every 6 hours by default. Pro customers can change the refresh interval to as often as every 15 minutes.
