# Acme Analytics Security FAQ

All customer data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher. Data is stored in the region the customer selects when creating the workspace: EU, US or Middle East.

Acme Analytics supports single sign-on through SAML 2.0 on the Pro plan. Two-factor authentication is available to every plan and can be made mandatory by a workspace Admin.

The company completed a SOC 2 Type II audit in 2025. Audit reports are available to customers under NDA by emailing the security team.

Customer data is never used to train machine learning models. When a workspace is deleted, all associated data is permanently removed from production systems within 30 days and from backups within 90 days.

Support is available by chat from 8am to 8pm Gulf Standard Time, Sunday to Thursday. Pro customers also receive a guaranteed first response within 4 business hours.
