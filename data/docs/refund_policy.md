# Acme Analytics Refund Policy

Customers on a monthly plan may request a full refund within 14 days of their first payment. After 14 days, monthly plans are not refundable, but the customer can cancel at any time and will keep access until the end of the billing period.

Annual plans have a longer window. Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, a prorated refund is available only if the account has had a documented service outage of more than 24 hours.

Refund requests must be sent to the billing team through the in-app Billing page. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 7 business days. Refunds are not available for add-on credits that have already been consumed.
